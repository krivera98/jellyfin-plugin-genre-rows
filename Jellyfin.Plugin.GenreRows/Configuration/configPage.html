Genre Rows

Adds one Home Screen Sections row per genre, such as "Comedy Movies" or "Drama Shows". After saving, turn the rows on and put them in order in the Home Screen Sections settings, like any other section.

Comma-separated, e.g. Action, Comedy, Animation. Leave empty to use your biggest genres automatically.
Comma-separated. These never get a row.
Saving updates the rows straight away. Rows for genres you remove stay listed in Home Screen Sections until Jellyfin restarts.
